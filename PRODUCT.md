# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations staff use CaifuClaw AI to manage multi-platform ecommerce fulfillment across order intake, purchasing, inventory, printing, shipping labels, reporting, and configuration. They work in a dense admin environment where fast scanning, predictable controls, and reliable status feedback matter more than decorative presentation.

## Product Purpose

CaifuClaw AI aggregates marketplace order fulfillment workflows into one business application. Success means platform data is synchronized reliably, operators can move orders through purchasing and outbound steps with fewer manual handoffs, and managers can inspect logs, settings, exchange rates, and operational health from a consistent interface.

## Brand Personality

Practical, orderly, dependable. The interface should feel focused and familiar, with CaifuClaw adding a helpful assistant layer without disrupting the operational flow.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing page composition, decorative dashboards, low-density novelty UI, heavy animation, and custom controls that make standard admin workflows harder to recognize.

## Design Principles

- Preserve operator focus with dense but legible layouts.
- Prefer familiar admin patterns over novelty.
- Make state and failure conditions visible at the point of work.
- Keep AI assistance formatted, readable, and secondary to the task.
- Use restrained color for navigation, action, and status meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast for operational text, and motion only when it communicates status. Reduced-motion users should not lose information.
